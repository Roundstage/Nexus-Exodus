# Earth natural landmarks — 6 September 2026

Current user direction: preserve the western city; remove remaining isolated buildings and terrain stairways. Wilderness landmarks should explain the shape of the terrain and offer recognizable places to explore.

| Reference | Observation | Application to this map |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RPG Maker: Mapping Cliffs](https://www.rpgmakerweb.com/blog/tips-and-tricks-mapping-cliffs) | Consistent cliff height, irregular outlines, matching rubble and plausible routes make elevation readable. | Replace long straight cliff stripes and grass stairs with coherent outcrops. Keep open routes around their feet. |
| [RPG Maker: How to Map a Desert](https://www.rpgmakerweb.com/blog/how-to-map-a-desert) | Distinct landmarks help players orient themselves; too much clutter obscures the desert character. | One sandstone massif, a cave and a small dune field, with substantial open sand between formations. |

These are design interpretations of the sources, not a physical erosion simulation. Reference artwork/photos are not copied into the game; the new game artwork is original image generation with retained native sources. Review uses actual DMI pixels at 32px tile scale. StrongDMM remains deferred.
